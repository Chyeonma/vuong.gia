# Tài nguyên

- `vuong-gia.webp`: chân dung dùng trên trang (149 KB), tối ưu từ ảnh gốc.
- `portrait-original.png`: ảnh gốc, cũng dùng cho thẻ chia sẻ Open Graph.
- `favicon.svg`: biểu tượng hoa thị của website, được vẽ bằng SVG.
- `fonts/`: phông chữ tự lưu trong dự án; giấy phép SIL Open Font License đi kèm.

Chân dung là nhân vật hư cấu, được tạo bằng công cụ **imagegen tích hợp**, không dùng CLI/API. Lời nhắc cuối cùng:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: vertical editorial portrait for a fictional Vietnamese personal portfolio, subject named Vương giả. Primary request: timeless, candid black-and-white portrait of a fictional Vietnamese creative man in his late twenties, wavy medium black hair, clean shaven, relaxed thoughtful expression looking slightly to the left of the camera, wearing a textured dark charcoal blazer over a plain black crewneck. Scene: outdoors beside a pale warm concrete wall, subtle out-of-focus bamboo leaves casting a quiet shadow at top right. Composition: vertical 4:5 portrait from waist up, subject sitting comfortably on a low concrete bench with hands loosely together, human and approachable, subject centered with generous headroom. Style: refined independent magazine photography, real skin texture, cinematic analog 35mm film grain, elegant soft natural side light, rich charcoal blacks, warm ivory highlights, medium contrast, understated artistic mood. No typography, no text, no logos, no watermark. Create a standalone photographic asset, not a website mockup.
